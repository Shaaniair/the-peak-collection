## What Is a Candle Warmer Lamp?

A candle warmer lamp uses heat from a warming bulb to melt candle wax without requiring an open flame.

## Why People Consider Them

A candle warmer can combine fragrance, ambient lighting and decorative appeal in one product.

## Features to Look For

Depending on the product, useful features may include:

- Adjustable brightness
- Timer functionality
- Adjustable height
- Suitable candle size
- Stable base
- Attractive design
